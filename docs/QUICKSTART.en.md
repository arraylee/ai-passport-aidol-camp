1. Install the same firmware on your badges. After the welcome message, press Down briefly to activate. Everything runs offline; no network setup is needed.
2. Use Up/Middle to select and Down to confirm. Choose Team on the home screen to join the room. Select everyone or a teammate, hold Up to talk, and release to finish.
3. To add a friend, bring two badges close together on their home screens and have both people press Down when the avatar appears. If it does not appear, open Friends on both badges to search.
4. Choose Play Game in the room. Hum a comfortable low note during preparation, keep making sound to move, and raise your pitch to jump higher. Pause on platforms to breathe; reach the flag to finish.
5. Press Down briefly to leave a game, or hold Down to return home and leave the room. After 20 idle minutes the badge sleeps; press Up to wake it.
